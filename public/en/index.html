BEFORE YOU JOIN

Good to know.

Do I need to be a student?

Builders and wildcard evaluators must be 18 or older and currently enrolled in college. Builders study a computing-related course. Any adult can take part as a pain finder.

What makes a good problem?

Start with something you or someone you know really experiences. Explain who struggles, what they do today and one result they could test in a week. Keep private details out of the brief.

What do builders submit?

A working website, a public source repository with build-week commit history and a three to four minute YouTube demo. Disclose templates, libraries and AI tools you use, along with applicable licences.

How are projects scored?

In the proposed rubric, the problem author scores usefulness, ease of use and willingness to keep using the solution out of 50. Judges score reliability, fit, quality, originality and clarity out of 50. Peer scores are for learning.

Will I get a certificate?

Participants who complete their role can receive a digital certificate with a unique ID.

Use the certificate checker to verify an issued certificate.

What can I share publicly?

Keep your match anonymous until the reveal. Use sample data and leave personal, financial or confidential details out of briefs, websites and videos. You can ask questions through the organizers.

What would you fix?

Someone else might have the fresh eyes it needs.
